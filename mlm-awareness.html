CYBERSECURITY

Think like an investigator.

Online opportunities should be evaluated with the same mindset used when assessing digital risks: verify information, identify inconsistencies and avoid trusting claims without evidence.

Verify

Check the company, registration, products, compensation plan and public information.

Question

Ask where the money actually comes from and who ultimately pays the participants.

Evidence

Separate documented evidence from testimonials, screenshots and claims made during recruitment.

SCAM AWARENESS

Common warning signs.

🚩 Unrealistic Income

Claims that ordinary people can quickly make extremely large amounts of money.

🚩 Recruitment Pressure

Pressure to recruit friends, family or colleagues rather than focusing on genuine customers.

🚩 Urgency

Statements such as "join today", "this opportunity will disappear" or "don't miss your chance."

🚩 Discouraging Questions

Criticism is dismissed as negativity rather than being addressed with evidence.

MLM AWARENESS

MLM is not automatically a Ponzi scheme.

Multi-Level Marketing generally involves selling products or services through a network of participants. However, individual companies must be evaluated based on how their actual business and compensation structures work.

MLM

Participants may earn money from product sales and, depending on the compensation plan, sales generated by people they recruit.

Pyramid Scheme

The central economic activity is recruitment, with money flowing through new participants rather than genuine demand for products or services.

Ponzi Scheme

Returns to earlier participants are funded by money from newer participants rather than genuine investment profits.

RED FLAGS

Questions worth asking.

Where does the money come from?

Is revenue primarily generated from genuine external customers or from participants?

What happens if recruitment stops?

Consider whether the business can remain financially sustainable without continuously adding new participants.

Can the product stand alone?

Would people purchase the product at its normal price without joining the opportunity?

Are income claims documented?

Ask for verifiable income disclosures rather than relying solely on testimonials.
